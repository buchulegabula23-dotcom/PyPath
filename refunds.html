Refund Policy

Last updated 19 September 2026

We want you to feel safe trying PyPath. Here is how refunds work.

Free trial

Your first 7 days are free. Cancel before the trial ends and you pay nothing.

Money-back window

If you're not happy after your first payment, email us within 7 days of that charge and we'll refund it in full. This applies to both the monthly and the annual plan.

After that

You can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the period you paid for. We don't give partial refunds for unused time after the 7-day window.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to your original payment method through Lemon Squeezy and usually appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

How to ask

Email pypathcourse@gmail.com with the email address you used to subscribe.

This policy doesn't affect any rights you have under South African consumer law.
