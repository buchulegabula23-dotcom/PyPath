Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 September 2026

This policy explains how PyPath, operated by Buchule Gabula, handles your personal information under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What we collect

  • Your name and email address, when you sign up or check out.
  • Payment details, which are collected and stored by Lemon Squeezy, not by us.
  • Basic technical data (such as your IP address) that our hosting provider records when you visit.

How we use it

To give you access to the course, send receipts and important account messages, answer your questions and improve the course. We do not sell your information.

Who we share it with

Lemon Squeezy (payments, tax and receipts) and GitHub (website hosting). Fonts on this site load from Google Fonts. These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

On your device

This site saves your name, email and trial status in your own browser so it can show your trial. It stays on your device, and clearing your browser data removes it. We don't use advertising cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your details while you are a subscriber and for as long as the law requires afterwards.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Email pypathcourse@gmail.com and we will respond promptly. You may also complain to South Africa's Information Regulator.
